THE PROJECT

From pixels to better choices.

EcoVision AI is a transparent educational project: the app only predicts after a real model has been trained and saved.

Workflow

  1. PrepareTrashNet class folders are split into train, validation, and test sets without leakage.
  2. LearnMobileNetV2 transfer learning learns visual patterns from the training images.
  3. CheckTest accuracy, per-class precision/recall/F1, and a confusion matrix are generated.
  4. GuideA confidence threshold prevents overconfident-looking low-quality answers.

Responsible use

Confidence is an estimate, not proof. Lighting, occlusion, unusual items, and dataset bias can affect results.

Disposal rules differ by location. Always check local council guidance, especially for batteries, electronics, chemicals, and broken glass.
